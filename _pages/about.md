Hello! My name is Li Liu, a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science and Engineering department at University of California, Santa Cruz. I am privileged to be advised by [Prof. Leilani Gilpin](https://people.ucsc.edu/~lgilpin/). I obtained my Master's and Bachelor's degree from Zhejiang University in China. My research focuses on spatial understanding and behavioral modeling. I develop assistive technologies that enhance human perception and adapt to users’ behaviors and needs. My goal is to connect perception, reasoning, and interaction in human-centered systems.
