Short description:
Tactile Map Annotation: On a tactile map, a building under the finger is an unnamed shape. Our tool gives it a name in Braille.
Ten blind and low-vision readers compared labeled and unlabeled prints. Each label also links to a real place, so it can drive an audio readout.
